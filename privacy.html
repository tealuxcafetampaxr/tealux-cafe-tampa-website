Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

Tealux Cafe Tampa ("we," "us," or "our") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you visit our website or interact with our services.

Information We Collect

We may collect the following types of information:

Phone number — only when you voluntarily provide it to receive a photo or text message from us. We do not store your phone number after the message is delivered.

Usage data — basic, anonymized data about how visitors use our website (e.g., pages visited). This data is not linked to any personal identity.

How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect solely to deliver the service you requested — such as sending you a photo or responding to an inquiry. We do not use your information for marketing without your explicit consent.

SMS / Text Messages

If you opt in to receive an SMS from us, you will receive one (1) message per session. Message and data rates may apply. You can opt out at any time by replying STOP. For help, reply HELP or contact us at info@tealuxcafetampa.com.

We do not sell, rent, or share your phone number with third parties for marketing purposes.

Third-Party Services

Our website may contain links to third-party services such as DoorDash, Uber Eats, Instagram, and Facebook. These services have their own privacy policies, and we are not responsible for their practices. We encourage you to review their policies before providing personal information.

Cookies

This website does not use tracking cookies or advertising pixels. No personal data is collected through automated means.

Data Retention

We do not retain personal data beyond what is necessary to fulfill the immediate service requested. Phone numbers used for SMS delivery are not stored in any database.

Children's Privacy

Our services are not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your Rights

You have the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of any personal information we may hold. To make a request, contact us at info@tealuxcafetampa.com.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date. Continued use of our website constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
