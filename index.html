Welcome to Tealux Cafe Tampa

Tealux Cafe Tampa is your go-to spot for handcrafted boba, milk teas, and desserts in Tampa, FL. Every drink is made with care — from rich brown sugar milk teas to fresh fruit blends. Stop in, stay a while, and sip something good. We're open every day.
